Privacy Policy

Last updated : October 17, 2026

In short

BossTycoon is played without an account. We (AMS STUDIO) do not collect, receive or sell any personal data: your game stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your saved game, your settings (language, sound) and the list of your purchases are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They may be included in device backups managed by Apple (iCloud, if you turned it on). We have no access to them. Deleting the game erases them.

In-app purchases

Purchases (ecus, Boss Pass, decorations) are paid for and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email address or payment details: the game only receives confirmation that an item was bought. See Apple's privacy policy.

Video ads

The game offers videos only when you choose to watch one to get a boost. They are served by the Unity LevelPlay ad mediation platform (Unity Technologies) and its partner networks.

These ads are non-personalised: the game does not ask for Apple's tracking permission, does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and tells ad networks not to show targeted ads.

To display the video, confirm it was watched, avoid repeating the same ad and prevent fraud, the ad platform and its partners may process technical data: IP address, device model and OS version, language, the app-vendor identifier (IDFV) and interactions with the ad. See Unity's privacy policy.

What we don't do

No account, no cross-app tracking, no targeted advertising, no sale of data, no third-party analytics.

Children

BossTycoon is meant for a general audience. We collect no data, and ads are non-personalised for every player.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or erase data about you, and object to its processing, by writing to contact@ams-studio.lu. You can also contact the CNPD (Luxembourg's data protection authority).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
