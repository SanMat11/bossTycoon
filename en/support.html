Support

A question or a problem? Write to us at contact@ams-studio.lu: we reply within 48 business hours. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and the game version.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my game saved?

On your device, automatically every game day and when you leave the game. If you delete BossTycoon, your game is erased.

I have a new iPhone: how do I get my purchases back?

Open the Shop, Decoration or Ecus tab, and tap “Restore purchases”. Your Boss Pass and decorations come back. Ecus that were already spent cannot be restored.

I paid but received nothing.

Restart the game with an Internet connection: the purchase is delivered automatically. Ecus go to the company that is open. If nothing arrives, write to us or request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

No video is available.

Videos depend on the ad networks: try again a little later, with an Internet connection. With the Boss Pass, boosts are available without a video.

Is the game available in my language?

BossTycoon is available in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. It follows your device language, and you can change it in the game settings.
